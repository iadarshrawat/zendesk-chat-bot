Section 4 - First Aid Measures

 4.1 Description of first aid measures
 In all cases of doubt, or when symptoms persist, seek medical attention
 IF INHALED: Remove victim to fresh air and keep at rest in a position comfortable for breathing.
 Call a POISON CENTER or doctor/physician if you feel unwell.
 IF ON SKIN (or hair):Remove/Take off immediately all contaminated clothing. Rinse skin with water/shower.
 IF ON SKIN: Wash with plenty of soap and water. Call a poison center or doctor/physician if you feel unwell. Take
 off contaminated clothing and wash before reuse. If skin irritation occurs: Get medical advice/attention.
 IF IN EYES: Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses, if present and easy to do.
